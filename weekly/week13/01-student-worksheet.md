## Reading

依 2026-11-11 官方資訊改寫；新作日期是目前預定日期。粗體句先理解意思，讀完再學句型。

Some games tell players exactly where to go and what to do next. GTA V gives them another kind of world. A player can follow the story, but there are also roads to drive on, places to see, and things to try. The city feels like more than a road between two parts of a story. It becomes a place where players can spend time, even when they are not moving the story forward. Having that choice is part of what makes this kind of game interesting.

**GTA V was first released in 2013.** Its story takes players to Los Santos and the land around it. Instead of following just one person, players can move between three people and see different sides of the same story. Think of watching a movie and then being able to see what another person was doing at the same time. For a player, changing people can also change the way a place or a problem looks. The world is not just large; there are different ways to see it.

Now Rockstar Games, the company behind GTA, plans to release GTA VI on November 19, 2026. The new story follows Jason and Lucia in and around Vice City. For people who liked the old game, a new place and new people bring a question: what will it feel like to spend time there? A date tells us when the company plans to release a game. It does not tell us how good that game will be.

That is the real test for the new game. A large world may get people to look, but will they want to stay? A popular old game can make people interested in the future. The new game still has to give them its own reasons to care.

## 新文法：被動語態 be + p.p.

**把「接受動作的人／物」放到主詞位置。** p.p. 是過去分詞，和上週完成式用同一種動詞形態，但前面的字不同。

| 看誰做事：主動 | 看東西受到什麼動作：被動 |
| --- | --- |
| Rockstar Games released GTA V in 2013. | GTA V **was released** by Rockstar Games in 2013. |
| Many people play this game. | This game **is played** by many people. |

現在：**am / is / are + p.p.**；過去：**was / were + p.p.**。要交代誰做的才加 **by + 人／公司**。

別混淆：**has released** 已經發行（完成式）；**was released** 被發行（被動）。常用三態：make → made → made；play → played → played；release → released → released。

1. GTA V ______ released in 2013.（is / was）
2. These games ______ played by many people every day.（is / are）
3. The toy car was ______ by my brother.（make / made）
4. 改成被動：My brother made this game. → This game ______________________________.
5. 修正：The game released by the company in 2013. → ______________________________.
6. 填入 **has / was**：The company ______ released the game. / The game ______ released by the company.
