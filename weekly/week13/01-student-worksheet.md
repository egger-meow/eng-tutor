## Reading

依 2026-11-11 官方資訊改寫；新作日期是目前預定日期。粗體句先理解意思，讀完再學句型。

Some games tell players exactly where to go and what to do next. GTA V gives them another kind of world. It allows players to follow the story or spend time doing other things. There are roads to drive on, places to see, and things to try. The city feels like more than a road between two parts of a story. It becomes a place where players can spend time, even when they are not moving the story forward. Having that choice is part of what makes this kind of game interesting.

**GTA V was first released in 2013.** Its story takes players to Los Santos and the land around it. Players control three main characters, not just one. They can move between these characters and see different sides of the same story. Think of watching a movie and then being able to see what another person was doing at the same time. For a player, changing characters can also change the way a place or a problem looks. The world is not just large; there are different ways to see it.

Now Rockstar Games, the company behind GTA, plans to release GTA VI on November 19, 2026. The new story follows Jason and Lucia in and around Vice City. People who liked the old game may expect another interesting world. But what will the new experience feel like? A date tells us when the company plans to release a game. It does not tell us how good that game will be.

That is the big question for the new game. A large world may get people to look, but will they want to stay? An old game can make people interested in what comes next. The new game still has to give them its own reasons to care.

## 被動語態：先看「誰做」，再看「誰被做」

### 1. 同一件事，換一個主詞

**Rockstar Games released GTA V in 2013.** 公司發行遊戲：主詞是「做動作的人」。

**GTA V was released by Rockstar Games in 2013.** 遊戲被公司發行：主詞是「接受動作的東西」。

兩句發生的事相同，只是談話焦點不同。被動常用來介紹作品、物品或結果；不一定知道、也不一定需要說誰做的。

### 2. 主動改被動：四步一起看

原句：**My brother made this game.**（我哥哥做了這個遊戲。）

1. 找「被做的東西」：made 後面的 **this game**，移到句首當新主詞。
2. 依**新主詞與原句時間**選 be：this game 是單數，made 表示過去 → **was**。
3. 動詞換成過去分詞 p.p.：make → made → **made**；不能只移位置卻忘了 be。
4. 原來的動作者放在 **by** 後面：**by my brother**；時間、地點照原意保留。

結果：**This game was made by my brother.**（這個遊戲是我哥哥做的。）

注意：**Many people play this game. → This game is played by many people.** be 看新的主詞 this game，不看原來複數的 many people。

### 3. be 決定時間與單複數；後面固定用 p.p.

| 時間 | 單數主詞 | 複數主詞 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 現在經常發生 | This game **is played** every day. | These games **are played** every day. |
| 過去發生 | This game **was made** last year. | These games **were made** last year. |

主詞 I：現在用 **am**、過去用 **was**；you：現在用 **are**、過去用 **were**。

**p.p. 不等於一律加 -ed。** release → released → released；make → made → made；write → wrote → **written**。例如：The story **was written** by my sister. 不能用 was wrote。

### 4. 什麼時候需要 by？不是每句都要加

**This game was made by my brother.** 想說「誰做的」，保留 by my brother。

**This game was released in 2013.** 重點是遊戲與發行時間，可以不提公司。不能因為沒有 by，就認為不是被動。

by 後若用代名詞，要用受格：**by me / him / her / us / them**。例如 He made it. → It was made **by him**. 不是 by he。

### 5. 否定與問句：直接操作 be

- 否定：be 後加 **not**。The game **was not released** in 2012.（也可寫 wasn't。）
- 問句：把 be 移到主詞前。**Was the game released in 2013?**
- 短答：**Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.** 用 be 回答，不用 did。

### 6. 與上週完成式分開看

| 句子 | 結構與意思 |
| --- | --- |
| The company **has released** the game. | have/has + p.p.：公司已經發行遊戲，主詞仍是做事者。 |
| The game **was released** in 2013. | be + p.p.：遊戲被發行，主詞接受動作。 |

**看到 p.p. 先看前面是 have/has 還是 be。** 過去時間不等於只寫過去式：The game released by the company. 缺少被動需要的 **was**。也不是所有句子都能硬改被動：He sleeps. 沒有「被睡的東西」，不能寫成 He is slept.
