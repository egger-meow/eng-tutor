### A. From 66 to 68

In December 2025, a news **report** said that In-N-Out was no longer using 67 as a food order number. Workers said the numbers went from 66 to 68.

Why? “Six-seven” was **popular** online. Some young people wanted to hear the number at the store. When workers called it, they shouted and laughed. People could also **share** videos of this online.

The **meaning** of “six-seven” is not easy to **explain**. People do not always use it to mean the same thing. For some, saying it together is part of the fun. But workers still need to call numbers so people can get their food.

## Reading B | Jonathan 的新挑戰

**以下四幕為教學虛構，不是新聞中的真人事件。** Jonathan 和 Kevin 在一家漢堡店等餐。

### B1. A Very Serious Plan

Jonathan and Kevin bought some food. Their number was 65. Kevin wanted **to eat**, but Jonathan had another plan.

“Let's wait for 67,” Jonathan said. “I can **control** my face. I won't laugh.”

Kevin looked at him. “Like your first day at school?”

“This time I'm **serious**.” Jonathan asked Kevin **not to laugh**. They decided **to wait** near the front. Jonathan put their number in his bag.

### B2. The Wrong Number

A worker called, “Sixty-five!” Jonathan did not **notice**. He was watching Kevin's face. Kevin kept **looking** at the numbers above the worker.

“Sixty-six!”

“Here it comes,” Jonathan said. Kevin started to smile. Jonathan held up one finger. No laughing.

“Sixty-eight!”

Kevin looked at Jonathan. “Did he eat seven?”

### B3. Please Explain

Jonathan asked the worker **to repeat** the number.

“Sixty-eight,” she said.

“What about 67?”

“We don't use it here.”

### B4. Food Wins Again

Jonathan looked at his food. He had made a **decision** to wait for a number that would never come.

“We can stop waiting now,” Kevin said. They sat down to eat. Kevin finished **eating** first and wrote on a piece of paper:

> **SERIOUS FACE: 0 / 1**<br>
> **FOOD: COLD**<br>
> **NEXT PLAN: LISTEN FOR OUR OWN NUMBER**

## Grammar | 先看前面的動詞

### 1. 想做、決定做、計畫做：to + 原形

| 前面的動詞 | 接法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| want | want to + 原形 | Kevin wants **to eat**. |
| decide | decide to + 原形 | They decided **to wait**. |
| plan | plan to + 原形 | I plan **to read** the report. |

**兩個位置分開管：** She **decides** to **wait**. 現在式第三人稱的 s 在 decides；to 後面還是原形。They **decided** to **wait**. 過去式在 decided，不把 wait 也改成 waited。

### 2. 喜歡做、一直做、做完：V-ing

| 前面的動詞 | 接法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| enjoy | enjoy + V-ing | We enjoy **watching** funny videos. |
| keep | keep + V-ing | Kevin kept **laughing**. |
| finish | finish + V-ing | He finished **eating**. |

這裡的 V-ing 是前面動詞需要的接法，**不是看到 V-ing 就一定是現在進行式**。

He **is laughing**. → be + V-ing，正在笑。<br>
He **keeps laughing**. → keep + V-ing，一直笑；不用再加 is。

### 3. 請某人做／不要做：ask + 人 + (not) to V

Jonathan asked **Kevin to wait**.<br>
Jonathan asked **Kevin not to laugh**.

否定的是「不要笑」，not 放在 to 前面。比較：**Jonathan didn't ask Kevin to laugh.** 是「Jonathan 沒有請 Kevin 笑」，意思不同。

### 4. 舊規則別丟掉：do / does / did / can 後接原形

Did he **laugh**? / Can he **wait**? / Does she **want** to **eat**?

> **三步判斷**：先找 do／does／did／can → 後面先用原形；再看 want／keep 等動詞 → 決定下一個動作接 to V 還是 V-ing。<br>
> Did he **want to eat**? 不是 Did he wanted to ate?

**拼字回想：** wait → waiting；make → making；stop → stopping。to 後用原形，不寫 to waiting。

### 加分挑戰：stop 後面不同，事情也不同

**stop + V-ing**：停止原本正在做的事。He stopped **laughing**. 他不笑了。<br>
**stop + to V**：停下原本的事，去做另一件事。They stopped **to eat**. 他們停下手邊的事，去吃東西。
