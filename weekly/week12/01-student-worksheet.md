## 3. Reading | A Car in Space

以下為真實歷史事件與基礎科學的教學改寫，不是本週新發射新聞。先讀整篇，再回頭找證據。粗體句可先按中文提示理解，稍後再學句型。

### A. A Very Different Road Trip

Cars usually stay on roads. They carry people to school, work, or home. But one red car took a very different trip. On February 6, 2018, SpaceX sent a Tesla Roadster into space on a rocket called Falcon Heavy. Something called Starman sat in the car. It looked like a person, but it was not a real driver. The car also stayed joined to the top part of the rocket. It did not fly away from that part and start driving by itself.

### B. A Test, Not a New Road

This was Falcon Heavy's first trip. The company used the car as something for the rocket to carry during the test. Sending it up was a way to test the rocket, not a way to test a new road for cars. Two side parts of the rocket came back and landed, but the middle part did not land safely. The car and the top part went on a path around the sun. They did not land on Mars. So the trip was more than a funny picture, but it did not show that every part of the project worked well.

### C. Why Does It Keep Moving?

Think about pushing a toy car across a floor. After your hand leaves it, the car keeps moving for a short time. Then it slows down. The floor and the air act against the way it moves. Far out in space, there is no road under a car and very little air to slow it down. Something can keep moving without someone pushing it all the time. However, that does not mean nothing can change the way it moves. The sun pulls on things around it. This pull changes the path of something moving past it. Moving ahead and being pulled toward the sun can go together: a path around the sun is possible. The car does not need to drive along that path as it would on a road.

### D. What Have We Learned?

**People have sent a car into space.**（到目前為止，人類已有這項經驗。）But that does not mean we can all drive to another world. A picture can show something real without explaining everything about it. When you read a report about a new project, try three simple questions: What did people test? What worked, and what did not? What does the report really show?

You can use these questions for your own work, too. Think of a paper plane. If it flies well once, will it fly well every time? If it falls, have you learned nothing? **You have learned something from the test.**（測試帶來的收穫與現在有關。）You can change one thing, try again, and see what happens. A good question can turn a strange picture into a chance to learn. That is useful for the future, both in space and in your classroom.

> 讀懂三個重點：測試火箭載運 ≠ 測試汽車駕駛；繞太陽 ≠ 登陸火星；持續移動 ≠ 完全沒有力作用。

資料依據：NASA〈5 Years Ago: First Flight of the Falcon Heavy Rocket〉（2023-02-06）；太空運動為基礎科學簡化說明。來源連結與簡化界線見教師指引。

## 5. Grammar | 現在完成式：連到現在

### 先複習上週：動詞後面接什麼？

R1. We decided ________ (read) the report.

R2. She enjoys ________ (learn) about space.

R3. The teacher asked us ________ (not / run) in the room.

### A. have / has + 過去分詞（p.p.）

這裡 have 不是「擁有」。現在完成式連結過去與現在：到現在的經驗、現在的結果；不能取代所有過去式。

| 用途 | 例句 | 意思 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 到現在的經驗 | I have seen the video. | 到現在，我有看過。 |
| 現在的結果 | She has finished the report. It is ready now. | 她已完成，現在可用了。 |
| 否定 | We have not tested this car. | 到現在還沒測過這台車。 |
| 問句 | Has he read the report? | 他看過報告了嗎？ |
| 短答 | Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't. | 用 has 回答，不用 does。 |

I / you / we / they → **have**；he / she / it → **has**。haven't = have not；hasn't = has not。

### B. 同一個動詞，三種樣子

| 原形 | 過去式 | 過去分詞 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| see / do | saw / did | seen / done |
| write / take | wrote / took | written / taken |
| send / make | sent / made | sent / made |
| read | read | read |
| test / finish | tested / finished | tested / finished |

read 的拼字三欄相同，但後兩欄念法像 red；本表由老師示範，不當發音遷移測驗。

### C. 有明確「過去時間」，改用過去式

| 想說什麼 | 正確例句 |
| --- | --- |
| 2018 年那次事件 | SpaceX sent the car into space in 2018. |
| 到現在已經有這個經驗 | People have sent a car into space. |
| 昨天看了 | I saw the video yesterday. |
| 看過，沒說是哪一天 | I have seen the video. |

不要寫 **I have seen it yesterday.** 本課的 yesterday、last night、in 2018 都指定已結束的過去時間。

**比較：** Did he **see** it?（原形）／Has he **seen** it?（p.p.）／Is he **watching** it?（V-ing）
