## 2. Reading: The Next Video

以下是**虛構的學生故事**。讀時標記：`F` = 故事中的事實，`I` = 人物的推想，`C` = 人物的選擇。

### Beat 1 — A good start

On Monday, Jay opens YouTube Shorts after dinner. He wants to see one video about drawing. The first video shows an easy way to draw a cat. Jay likes it and tries it on paper. Then another short video starts. It is funny. Jay says, “One more.”

### Beat 2 — Where did the time go?

Jay sees more videos: a game, a dog, and a new way to draw. He does not choose each one before it starts. When he looks at the time, it is 10:40. He wanted to read for ten minutes before bed, but now he feels tired. He puts his book down. “I didn't notice the time,” he says.

### Beat 3 — A talk, not a fight

The next day, his friend May asks, “Do short videos always affect your sleep?” Jay says, “I don't know. One night cannot tell us that. The drawing video helped me. But I often keep watching after I want to stop.” May says, “Maybe the next video makes it easy to stay. Let's compare two nights.” They do not say that every video is bad.

### Beat 4 — Two nights

On Tuesday, Jay sets a limit: fifteen minutes after dinner. When the time is up, he puts down his phone. He reads and goes to bed at 10:00. On Wednesday, he has no limit. He keeps watching until 10:35 and reads for only two minutes. On Thursday, he can focus on his homework better than he could on Wednesday. Jay thinks his night habit *may* influence his attention the next day. He knows two nights cannot show what happens to everyone.

| Night | Video time | Reading time | Bed time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday: with a limit | 15 minutes | 20 minutes | 10:00 |
| Wednesday: no limit | 50 minutes | 2 minutes | 10:35 |

### Beat 5 — His own plan

Jay does not want to avoid all short videos. Some teach him to draw and some make him laugh. He makes a choice he can control: “I can watch after dinner, but not before bed. I will take a break when my time is up.” He puts a book by his bed and asks May to check his plan next week. The plan is small. Jay wants to see if it helps.

## 5. Grammar: How often?

**問頻率：** How often do you watch short videos?  
**回答：** I **sometimes** watch them after dinner.

| 位置 | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一般動詞前 | 主詞 + 頻率副詞 + 動詞 | Jay **often watches** after dinner. |
| be 動詞後 | 主詞 + be + 頻率副詞 | Jay **is sometimes** tired. |
| 問句 | How often + do/does + 主詞 + 動詞原形? | How often **does** Jay **watch**? |

`always → usually → often → sometimes → never`（由多到少）。頻率副詞說的是**大約多常**，不是精確分鐘數。`does` 後動詞用原形：`does Jay watch`。

### A. Choose and fix

1. Jay ______ watches videos after dinner. (`often` / `is often`)  
2. Jay is ______ tired after a late night. (`sometimes` / `does sometimes`)  
3. How often ______ May watch videos? (`do` / `does`)  
4. How often does Jay ______ before bed? (`read` / `reads`)  
5. 改錯：Jay watches often videos. → __________________________________________  
6. 改錯：How often does Jay watches? → _______________________________________
