## Reading

<div class="week14-reading">

<p>A sea otter comes up to the top of the water with a rock. It lies on its back and puts the rock on the front of its body. Then it takes out some food with a hard shell and hits the rock with the food. After a few hits, the shell breaks. The otter can now eat the soft food inside. The rock stays on its body while it eats.</p>

<p>Where did the food come from? Sea otters look for food at the bottom of the sea, but they come up to eat. A sea otter that finds a snail can put it in a pocket under one of its front legs. The pocket is made of skin. With the first snail in its pocket, the otter can use both front legs to look for another one. It can bring more than one snail up at a time. It can also carry a small rock in the pocket.</p>

<p>Why use a rock when you have teeth? An otter can bite some shells open. But a shell that is too hard can hurt its teeth. With a rock, the otter can break open food it might not be able to open with its teeth. It can also use a shell with no food in it as a tool for opening other food.</p>

<p>In California, scientists watched 196 sea otters to learn about the tools they use. They found that sea otters that often used tools could eat bigger animals and animals with harder shells. The sea otters that used tools also had less damage to their teeth. A rock can change what an otter is able to eat and help keep its teeth from getting hurt.</p>

## B｜先圈名詞，再填字

<div class="week14-task">
<p>從 A1–A3 找規則。圈選「人」或「東西或動物」，再做 B1–B4。</p>
<p><strong>who</strong> 放在（人／東西或動物）後面。　 <strong>which</strong> 放在（人／東西或動物）後面。</p>
<p>這些用來指出「哪一個」的句子，也可以用 <strong>that</strong> 接在人、動物或東西後面。</p>
</div>

<div class="week14-task week14-fill">
<p><strong>B1.</strong> The girl __________ has the book is my sister.（who / which）</p>
<p><strong>B2.</strong> The rock __________ is on the table is wet.（who / which）</p>
<p><strong>B3.</strong> The sea otter that __________ the rock is on its back.（has / have）</p>
<p><strong>B4.</strong> The sea otters that __________ rocks are on their backs.（has / have）</p>
<p>B3、B4 填完再圈　<strong>that</strong> 後的動詞由（前面的名詞／後面的名詞）決定單複數。</p>
