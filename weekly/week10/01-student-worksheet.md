## Reading | 新聞 × 實況對話

先讀兩篇，再回答問題。**A 是官方新聞的簡化改寫；B 是為本課編寫的虛構對話。** 不需要玩過遊戲也能作答。

專有名稱只需認得：Minecraft（遊戲名）、The Sift（新維度名稱）、Java / Bedrock（遊戲版本名）、Overworld / Nether / End（原有三個維度）。這裡的「維度」指遊戲中的不同世界。

### A1. Big News

Minecraft players have some big **news**. At Minecraft Live on September 26, 2026, Mojang, the **company** behind the game, showed a new world called the Sift. It will join the Overworld, the Nether, and the End as Minecraft's fourth world.

### A2. Next Year, Not Today

The company's **report** says the Sift will come to Minecraft Java and Bedrock in 2027. It also says the Sift is part of Minecraft Dungeons II, a different game. Players should read the game name carefully. News about one game does not always mean the same thing for another game.

### A3. Read Before You Share

The report gives a year for Java and Bedrock, but it does not give a day or a month. This is news about the **future**. Before you **share** a video with a friend, ask two **simple** questions: Which game is it about? When can players play it?

來源：[Minecraft 官方公告，2026-09-26](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/mclive_sept2026_recap)。查核：2026-10-14。本文縮寫為簡易英文；遊戲未來安排仍可能變動。

### B. Two Friends, One Video

以下是虛構實況對話，不是官方功能預告。Leo 和 May 正在談一支新影片。

**Leo:** Look at this! The video says, “Play the fourth world now!” I will open Minecraft tonight. Will you play with me?

**May:** Wait. What is the name of the game in the video?

**Leo:** Oh, it says Minecraft Dungeons II. I play Minecraft Java. Are they the same game?

**May:** No. Let's read the company's report first. It says the Sift will come to Java next year.

**Leo:** So I won't find it in Java tonight. But I can still make a little world of my own. I will **create** a new map this weekend. There will be a small house near a blue river.

**May:** Nice! Will you put a big green animal in it?

**Leo:** Maybe! That's my idea, not news from the company. I will **test** the map with you first. Then I will **improve** it. What will you do?

**May:** I will make a short video. I won't say our map is the Sift. I will say, “This is our idea for a new world.”

**Leo:** Good. I'll share the video after we test the map. And I'll read the game name before I share other news!

### Say It | 字母 sh 的聲音

用 **share** 練習：嘴唇稍微往前，牙齒靠近、不要咬住，讓氣持續流出；不要在前面多加一個「四」的音，也不要在尾巴加「呃」。先把 sh 的氣流做出來，再接後面的聲音。

**只測試發音，不教意思、不算本週單字。老師先不要示範下面三字。** 看見相同的 sh，自己試讀：

**ship　　shop　　shed**

老師記錄：□ 能自行找出 sh　□ 氣流連續　□ 沒多加母音

## Grammar | 接下來會怎樣？

### 1. 本週新句型：will + 原形動詞

用來說未來的事、預測，或現在做的決定。新聞中的未來安排也常用 will；**看到 will 不代表事情已經發生，也不代表永遠不會改變。**

| 功能 | 句型 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 肯定 | 主詞 + will + 原形 | I **will read** the report. |
| 否定 | 主詞 + will not / won't + 原形 | He **won't share** it tonight. |
| 問句 | Will + 主詞 + 原形 ...? | **Will you play** with me? |
| 短答 | Yes, 主詞 + will. / No, 主詞 + won't. | Yes, I will. / No, I won't. |
| 問做什麼 | What + will + 主詞 + 原形 ...? | **What will you do** this weekend? |

**I will = I'll；will not = won't。he / she / it 後面也用 will，不加 s。**

<div class="callout warning">
<strong>先找前面有沒有「幫手」，再決定後面的動詞。</strong>
<p>He plays every day. → Does he <b>play</b> every day?</p>
<p>He will <b>play</b> tomorrow. → Will he <b>play</b> tomorrow?</p>
<p>不要寫 Does he plays? / He will plays. / Will he to play?</p>
</div>

### 2. 跟已學句型放在一起看

| 你想說什麼？ | 例句 | 看哪裡？ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 平常做 | He **plays** every day. | 一般現在式，he 後面加 s。 |
| 問平常做不做 | **Does** he **play** every day? | does 已經幫忙，play 用原形。 |
| 問昨天做了沒 | **Did** he **play** yesterday? | did 後面仍是原形。 |
| 此刻正在做 | He **is playing** now. | be + V-ing。 |
| 明天會做 | He **will play** tomorrow. | will + 原形。 |
| 已打算做 | He **is going to play** tomorrow. | be going to + 原形。 |

will 和 be going to 有時都可以談未來；本週先練會 will 的肯定、否定與問答，不把兩者硬分成完全不能互換。

### 3. There will be：將會有……

**There is a house now. → There will be a house next year.**

There will be a house. / There will be two houses.<br>
will 後面的 be 不變成 is 或 are。本週先會看懂、會套一句就好。
