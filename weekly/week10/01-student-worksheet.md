## Reading | 新聞 × 實況對話

先讀兩篇，再回答問題。**A 是官方新聞的簡化改寫；B 是為本課編寫的虛構對話。** 不需要玩過遊戲也能作答。

專有名稱只需認得：Minecraft（遊戲名）、The Sift（新維度名稱）、Java / Bedrock（遊戲版本名）、Overworld / Nether / End（原有三個維度）。這裡的「維度」指遊戲中的不同世界。

### A1. Big News

Minecraft players have some big **news**. At Minecraft Live on September 26, 2026, Mojang, the **company** behind the game, showed a new world called the Sift. It will join the Overworld, the Nether, and the End as Minecraft's fourth world.

### A2. Next Year, Not Today

The company's **report** says the Sift will come to Minecraft Java and Bedrock in 2027. It also says the Sift is part of Minecraft Dungeons II, a different game. Players should read the game name carefully. News about one game does not always mean the same thing for another game.

### A3. Read Before You Share

The report gives a year for Java and Bedrock, but it does not give a day or a month. This is news about the **future**. Before you **share** a video with a friend, ask two **simple** questions: Which game is it about? When can players play it?

來源：[Minecraft 官方公告，2026-09-26](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/mclive_sept2026_recap)。查核：2026-10-14。本文縮寫為簡易英文；遊戲未來安排仍可能變動。

### B. Two Friends, One Video

以下是虛構實況對話，不是官方功能預告。Leo 和 May 正在談一支新影片。

**Leo:** Look at this! The video says, “Play the fourth world now!” I will open Minecraft tonight. Will you play with me?

**May:** Wait. What is the name of the game in the video?

**Leo:** Oh, it says Minecraft Dungeons II. I play Minecraft Java. Are they the same game?

**May:** No. Because they are different games, let's read the company's report first. It says the Sift will come to Java next year.

**Leo:** So I won't find it in Java tonight. But I can still make a little world of my own. When I **create** a new map this weekend, I will put a small house near a blue river.

**May:** Nice! Will you put a big green animal in it?

**Leo:** Maybe! That's my idea, not news from the company. I will **test** the map with you first. Then I will **improve** it. What will you do?

**May:** If you have time, I will make a short video with you. This is our map, so I won't say it is the Sift. I will say, “This is our idea for a new world.”

**Leo:** Good. I'll share the video after we test and improve the map. And before I share other news, I'll read the game name!

## Grammar 1 | 原因、結果與先後順序

### 1. because 接原因；so 接結果

先想清楚「為什麼」和「所以怎樣」，再選連接詞。不能只看到兩個句子，就隨便放 because 或 so。

| 你先說什麼？ | 句型 | 同一件事的兩種說法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 先說結果，再補原因 | 結果 + because + 原因 | I won't share it **because I don't know the game name**. |
| 先說原因，再說結果 | 原因, so + 結果 | I don't know the game name, **so I won't share it**. |
| 原因放最前面 | Because + 原因, 結果 | **Because I don't know the game name**, I won't share it. |

**because 後面接「原因」；so 後面接「結果」。** 原因換到前面或後面，意思仍要對。

<div class="callout warning">
<strong>同一組因果，選一個接法，不把 because 和 so 一起塞進去。</strong>
<p>Because this is our map, <s>so</s> we will call it our idea.</p>
<p>This is our map, so we will call it our idea.</p>
</div>

### 2. before / after：不是背中文，要判斷誰先做

下列三句都表示：**先測試 → 再分享**。句子先寫哪個動作，不等於那個動作先發生。

| 句子 | 先做什麼？ |
| --- | --- |
| I will test the map **before I share it**. | 先 test，再 share。 |
| I will share the map **after I test it**. | 先 test，再 share。 |
| **Before I share the map**, I will test it. | 先 test，再 share。 |

**before / after + 主詞 + 動詞** 可以接一整件事；放句首時，這一段後面加逗號。

讀到 **I will share it after I test it.**，先圈 after，再用箭頭標出實際先後：________ → ________。

### 3. 把句子拆成兩塊看

**I will share the video | after we test the map.**

- 主要想說的安排：I will share the video.
- 補充什麼時間：after we test the map.

先找兩邊各自的主詞與動詞，長句就不必整串硬背。下一頁要處理：兩邊都談未來，為什麼只有一邊用 will？

## Grammar 2 | 未來的時間與條件

### 4. when / before / after：談未來，時間子句用現在式

「子句」就是句子裡一塊有自己主詞、動詞的部分。用 when / before / after 說明未來的時間時，**主要安排可用 will；時間子句用現在式**。

| 時間子句：現在式 | 主要安排：will + 原形 |
| --- | --- |
| **When Leo creates** the map this weekend, | May **will test** it. |
| **Before he shares** the video, | he **will read** the report. |
| **After they test** the map, | they **will improve** it. |

**When Leo creates ...** 的 creates 仍要看主詞 Leo；不能因為意思是未來，就寫成 When Leo will create ...。

### 5. if：先說「如果」，再說會怎樣

**If + 主詞 + 現在式, 主詞 + will + 原形。**

If May **has** time tomorrow, she **will test** the map.<br>
如果 May 明天有時間，她就會測試地圖。不是說她一定有時間。

If the map **doesn't work**, we **will improve** it.<br>
doesn't 後面的 work 用原形；另一邊 will 後的 improve 也用原形。

### 6. if 和 when：你是在說條件，還是在說時間？

| 句子 | 說話者的意思 |
| --- | --- |
| **If** May has time, she will play. | 如果有空才會玩；先看條件是否成立。 |
| **When** May gets home, she will play. | 說的是回家那個時間點；接著會玩。 |

不是所有 when 後面都不能有 will！下面是**直接問什麼時候**，沒有另一個主要安排：

**When will you share the video?**（你什麼時候會分享？）<br>
**I will share it when I get home.**（我回家時會分享。這裡才是時間子句。）

<div class="callout">
<strong>三步檢查，不靠中文逐字翻譯</strong>
<p>① 先看關係：原因、結果、時間，還是條件？</p>
<p>② 找兩邊的主詞、動詞；時間／條件子句用現在式，he/she/it 要留意 s。</p>
<p>③ 主要安排用 will + 原形。句首的時間／條件子句後加逗號。</p>
</div>

舊句型快速提醒：Does he **play**? / Did he **play**? / He will **play**.；助動詞後原形仍要保留，但不再當整課主軸。
