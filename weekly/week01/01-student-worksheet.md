## Part 1：閱讀短文

Jonathan just joined a new gaming forum. A user named Kevin sends him a message. Read it and answer the questions below.

> Hi! My name **is** Kevin. I **am** 13 years old, and I **am** a student at Riverside Middle School. I **am** not very tall, but I **am** pretty fast — that helps a lot in games!
>
> I **have** a small family. My father **is** a cook, and my mother **is** a teacher. I **have** one sister. She **is** ten years old, and she **loves** cartoons.
>
> On weekends, I **play** games with my friends online. We **like** building things together, and sometimes we **watch** funny videos after we finish a game. My favorite game **is** a building game — you **create** your own world block by block! It **is** so much fun.
>
> I also **watch** a lot of anime. My favorite show **is** about a school for fighters who battle scary monsters. The main hero **is** not the strongest at first, but he **is** very brave. He **gets** stronger every episode!
>
> I **am** a little busy this week because of homework, but I still **play** games every night. What about you? What **do** you like to do on weekends?
>
> — Kevin

## Part 3：文法練習 Grammar Practice

### 文法小提醒：be動詞後面接什麼？

**be動詞（am/is/are）後面接「名詞」或「形容詞」，用來說明主詞是誰、是什麼、或怎麼樣 —— 不是「受詞」喔！**

| 句型 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| 主詞 + be動詞 + **名詞** | I am **a student**. / My father is **a cook**. |
| 主詞 + be動詞 + **形容詞** | It is **so much fun**. / He is very **brave**. |
